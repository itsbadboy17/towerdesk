Saved Logins

Tried automatically when you add a device

Save the few logins and SNMP communities you use across your gear once. When you add or scan devices, TowerDesk tries them for you and picks the one that works — no need to choose a method or type a password per device. Stored encrypted; never shown again after saving.

What do the status words mean?
Live data
Logged in (or SNMP) and readings such as signal and traffic are arriving.
Starting…
The login works; the first readings are still coming (about 20 seconds).
Ping only
Answers ping, but TowerDesk has no login for it. Open the device and press Connect.
Wrong login
It answered but rejected the saved username/password.
Not answering
Ping works but its login page did not answer in time. Not a wrong password: the device is slow or the link to it loses packets.
Offline
No reply at all.
