Set up a radio

Type a radio's IP address, say what it will be, and TowerDesk shows exactly what would change before anything is written. Customer radios, sectors and point-to-point links are supported. MikroTik is a preview for now.

Reach it by MAC address

Plug the new MikroTik into the same switch as one you already have. That MikroTik (the relay) can see it by its MAC address and log in to it, even without an IP. TowerDesk gives it an address, turns on its API and sets its name. Then you add it and set up its radio below.
